My story

How I got here.

It started with a sewing machine and a pandemic spring break.

01

A mask business

During COVID-19 I started sewing masks in playful fabrics for my community. I built a system, formed a team, and made more than 600 masks in three months. The business earned $3,000 in profit, and I donated $1,500 to No Kid Hungry.

02

Learning business

That first taste of business led me to the International Business and Marketing Academy at Travis High School. I spent summers in the Wharton Global Youth Program, Harvard's Business Entrepreneurship Academy, and Boston University's Academic Immersion, learning marketing and strategy alongside international students.

03

Finding my voice

In my sophomore year I auditioned for The BEN Show, Blue Energy Nation's weekly podcast that educates young adults about the energy industry without political bias. I also sat on its advisory board, helping plan promotion, social media, and listener contests.

“I want to use business to connect communities, build relationships, and inspire others.”
